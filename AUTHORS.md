## Creator and maintainer

Python Study Guide was created and is maintained by [Ramon Estevez Rodriguez](https://github.com/RamonRDR).

The maintainer is responsible for the project's educational direction, editorial standards, repository organization, review decisions, and final approval of changes incorporated into the `main` branch.

## Community contributions

Community contributions are welcome. The authorship of individual changes remains preserved through commit metadata, Git history, and pull requests.

Project-level attribution does not erase or replace the credit of contributors who improve documentation, examples, translations, tests, or other parts of the repository.

## AI-assisted development

ChatGPT and Codex may assist with planning, research, drafting, translation, programming, testing, and review. AI assistance does not replace human responsibility or become automatic authorship of accepted content.

The maintainer remains responsible for understanding, verifying, reviewing, and approving material before it reaches the `main` branch.

## Attribution principle

When reusing or distributing substantial portions of this project, preserve the copyright and license notice required by the [MIT License](LICENSE). When contributing to this repository, preserve existing project attribution and allow Git and pull request history to record each contribution accurately.
