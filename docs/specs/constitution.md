# Python Study Guide Constitution

Status: proposed for PR review. Phase II direction approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-25.

## Principles

- Study. Understand. Practice. Technical accuracy and educational clarity take precedence over speed.
- Preserve the independent public MIT-licensed guide, existing authorship, identity, history, paths, examples and completed curriculum.
- The repository is the single source of truth for educational content; the external Learning Hub is a consumer, not a competing authoring source.
- Student-facing content must remain conceptually aligned in English, Brazilian Portuguese and Spanish. Engineering specifications use English.
- Students should practice manual programming, reasoning, debugging and self-assessment. AI-assisted maintenance remains subject to human review; no AI interviewer or semantic AI grading is in scope.
- Zero additional financial investment: reuse existing infrastructure and open-source tools; reassess rather than introduce paid services.
- Keep solutions original, generic and non-confidential; follow [repository instructions](../../AGENTS.md) and [license](../../LICENSE).
- Prefer simple, incremental, testable changes. No arbitrary restructuring, unnecessary dependencies or duplicate sources.
- Every change requires a focused branch, evidence, review and explicit maintainer approval before merge. Do not modify main directly, bypass protections or fabricate validation.

## Governance

[AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) controls repository-wide contributor behavior. This constitution supplements it; if requirements conflict, stop and seek maintainer resolution. Changes to these principles require a reviewed specification PR. Educational phases and engineering sprints are distinct; preserve historical Phase 0 (Project Foundation).

See [Phase II specification](learning-hub-foundation/specification.md).
